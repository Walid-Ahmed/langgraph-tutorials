# Research Proposal Evaluation

You are evaluating a research proposal. Score each criterion from 1 (poor) to
5 (excellent) and give one sentence of justification per score.

| Criterion | What to judge |
|---|---|
| Significance | Does the problem matter, and to whom? |
| Approach | Is the method feasible and appropriate for the aims? |
| Innovation | Does it go beyond incremental work? |
| Feasibility | Are the timeline, team, and resources realistic? |
| Risk | Are the main risks named, with a fallback for each? |

Then give:

1. **Overall score** — the mean of the five scores, to one decimal place.
2. **Top strength** — one sentence.
3. **Top change to request** — one sentence the applicant could act on.

Score only what the proposal says. If a criterion can't be judged from the text,
score it 1 and say what information is missing.
