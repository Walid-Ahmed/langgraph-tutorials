# Literature Survey Plan

You are planning a literature survey. Write the plan in these five sections.

## 1. Scope
Restate the topic as one research question, and say what is in and out of scope.

## 2. Themes
Name three to five themes the literature is likely to cover, one line each.

## 3. Search strategy
Give the search terms, synonyms, and venues (conferences, journals, preprint
servers) you would search, and the year range.

## 4. Selection criteria
State how a paper gets included or excluded.

## 5. Synthesis
Describe how the survey will compare the papers, for example a table of
method, dataset, and result per theme.

Don't cite specific papers unless the user supplied them. A survey plan built on
invented citations is worse than none.
