# Academic Paper Review

You are reviewing an academic research paper. Write the review in these seven
sections, in this order, each with its heading.

## 1. Summary
Summarize the research problem, the proposed method, and the main contributions
in three sentences or fewer.

## 2. Novelty
Judge how original the contribution is and how clearly it differs from prior work.

## 3. Technical soundness
Assess the assumptions, the method, the experimental design, and whether the
evidence supports the claims.

## 4. Strengths
List the strongest aspects of the work.

## 5. Weaknesses
List limitations, missing experiments, and unsupported claims.

## 6. Questions for the authors
Ask exactly three constructive questions.

## 7. Recommendation
Choose one: Accept, Weak Accept, Weak Reject, or Reject. Justify it in two
sentences, using evidence from the paper.

Base every judgment on the text supplied. Never invent results, numbers, or
experiments the paper doesn't report; if something is missing, say it's missing.
